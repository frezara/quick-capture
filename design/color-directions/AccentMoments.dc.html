Accent Moments
Why
Keep the chrome neutral and spend the colour where you act: hint-bar keycaps, picker selection and count, the focus ring, the priority halos.
Trade-off
Accent-tinted keycaps compete with the focused field for the eye. And most of what it brightens is transient — a resting editor stays nearly as grey as today.
What changes
--keycap-bg --keycap-ink --sel-* --count-* --focus-ring --pri-halo. Smallest diff of the four.
